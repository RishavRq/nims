FOR NIMA.

I still remember Darjeeling, and how it rained when we first met.

I remember the green kurta and the exact moment at 3:29 PM. I remember the ropeway trip that never happened.

I remember how much changed by August 24. And I remember the 20-hour visit. Carrying four heavy bags, the jump hug, and the Ferris wheel. I remember holding hands behind our parents, and that first kiss outside the church.

I am grateful for all those small, ordinary moments that existed between everything else.

I know things started to drift. I know there were misunderstandings, the dog note, and the silence. The hostel changed the shape of the distance between us.

I built this because I wanted a place to put all of these memories. I just wanted you to know how much I remembered, and that all of it mattered.

I know some things cannot be repaired through an interface.

You meant a lot to me. I am not asking for anything back, or for you to reply to this.

Happy birthday, Nima.
